# PROOFS.md — where the arguments are

The proofs are kept in their original typeset form rather than paraphrased
here; paraphrase is how a proof silently loses its hypotheses. This file maps
statement → argument.

## Audit

`../evidence/benchmarks/AUDIT_theorem_and_code_2026-08-01.md` is a line-level
audit of `proofs/eit_nogo_proofs.tex` (2026-08-01). Verdict: no mathematical
error across Theorems 1A–7B, lemmas and corollaries. Two documentation-level
gaps survive and are recorded in `LIMITATIONS.md`:

- **Theorem 1B has an unwritten step.** "A short rearrangement shows this
  equals …" hides a real subtlety: `L_μ|D⟩ = λ_μ|D⟩` gives
  `ρ_D L_μ† = λ_μ* ρ_D` but *not* `ρ_D L_μ = λ_μ* ρ_D`. A route via
  `H_eff := H − (i/2) Σ_μ L_μ†L_μ` avoids it.
- **T1 Theorem 2.1 is not proved here**, and Theorem 2.2's counterexample
  numbers are generated by no code here.
