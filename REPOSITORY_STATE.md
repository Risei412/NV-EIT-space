## What this repository is

Theory, proofs, and numerical verification for electromagnetically induced
transparency (EIT) in solid-state defect centers — starting from the NV center
in diamond and generalizing to a material-independent classification of
sector-mediated coherent response.

Three successive theories, each with its own proof document and its own
reproducible numerical campaign:

1. **Binary no-go (NV).** A transverse magnetic field opens a
   symmetry-suppressed Raman pathway quadratically (`K ∝ B⊥²`), producing an
   EIT-type response at finite temperature, while thermal dissipation
   suppresses it and defines a practical no-go boundary.
2. **Three-tier classification.** The binary go/no-go criterion is upgraded to
   an exclusive trichotomy of the suppression index
   `ν ∈ {∞} ∪ (0,∞) ∪ {0}` (exact structural no-go / asymptotic no-go /
   protected go) for finite-dimensional Markovian weak-probe systems. The
   theory itself and its material-independent campaigns live in a separate
   repository,
   [Sector-Master-Resolved-Theory](https://github.com/Risei412/Sector-Master-Resolved-Theory);
   what remains here are the NV-bound campaigns and the manuscript figures.
3. **Definition change.** EIT is redefined as *coherent cancellation in the
   observed response generated by the control field*, and the conditions
   equivalent to that definition are formulated and verified.

**Gate E (2026-08-02).** Requiring
`I_E = I_engineerable ∩ I_asymptotic ∩ I_detectable` to be non-empty:

- optical NV — **FAIL** (signal hits the detection floor before the asymptotic
  regime; 0.51–1.02 decade of usable window against 1 decade required);
- Gate B superconducting transfer — **FAIL** (effective exponent ≈ 0 at
  0.1–50 MHz; integer asymptotics 8+ orders away);
- 3-mode engineered-loss chain — **model-level conditional PASS**
  (class-3 global exponent 2.9002, MC 95% CI [2.8286, 2.9779], disjoint from
  class 2's [2.0680, 2.1090]).
