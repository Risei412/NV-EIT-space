# NV-EIT-space

Theory, proofs, and numerical verification for **electromagnetically induced
transparency (EIT) in solid-state defect centers** — starting from the NV
center in diamond and generalizing to a material-independent classification of
sector-mediated coherent response.

Transparency is defined here as *coherent cancellation in the observed response
generated by the control field*, carried by the sector cut
`Δχ_S = χ − χ^(S)_cut`, and systems are classified by a suppression index
`ν ∈ {∞} ∪ (0,∞) ∪ {0}` — exact structural no-go / asymptotic no-go /
protected go. The classification is believed established. Whether it is
*experimentally* meaningful is a separate question, and the answer so far is
largely negative: optical NV cannot have its exponent read at all.

**If you are an LLM or a script, start at `summary.json`.**
If you are a person, read `REPOSITORY_STATE.md`, then `CLAIMS.md` and
`NON_CLAIMS.md`.

Code lives in `calculations/`; everything it produces lives in `results/`,
routed by artifact kind — `.csv` and non-verdict `.json` to `tables/`, images
to `figures/`, `.npz` to `raw/`, and gate verdicts (`gate*.json`,
`gates_summary_*.json`) to `certificates/`. See `calculations/README.md`
before adding or moving anything.

## The three theories

1. **Binary no-go** — for NV centers, a transverse magnetic field opens a
   symmetry-suppressed Raman pathway quadratically (`K ∝ B⊥²`), producing an
   EIT-type response at finite temperature, while thermal dissipation
   suppresses it and defines a practical no-go boundary.
2. **Three-tier classification** — the binary criterion upgraded to an
   exclusive trichotomy in `ν`, with the finite exponent integer-valued.
   The theory itself and its material-independent campaigns live in
   [Sector-Master-Resolved-Theory](https://github.com/Risei412/Sector-Master-Resolved-Theory);
   what remains here are the NV-bound campaigns and the manuscript figures.
3. **Definition change** — EIT redefined response-theoretically, with the
   conditions equivalent to that definition formulated and verified, and a
   dark-state-free 2g+2e witness.

```bash
pip install -r "calculations/numerics/No-go theorem/requirements.txt"

```

## Conventions

- Every campaign writes a `gates_summary_*.json` recording each gate's numeric
  outcome and PASS/FAIL verdict. **Certificates outrank prose:** where a
  document and a certificate disagree, the certificate is right and the
  document is a defect to fix.
- Figures are traceable to the script and the gate summary that produced them;
  the figure regression tests enforce it.
- Failures are primary assets. They live in `evidence/failures/` and are cited
  from `NON_CLAIMS.md`; they are not deleted when a later gate succeeds.
- Documents are in English or Japanese depending on the working stage; the
  proof documents (`theory/proofs/*.tex`) are English throughout.
