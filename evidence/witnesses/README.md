# evidence/witnesses/

Minimal, self-contained systems that exhibit a claimed effect on their own,
independently of the campaign that motivated them.

| Witness | Contents |
|---|---|
| `2g2e_package/` | **Dark-state-free EIT in a 2g+2e system.** A complete package: analysis code, spectrum and Γ-scaling data, absorption / Γ-class / pole-zero figures, parameters, its own `SHA256SUMS.txt`, and reports in English and Japanese. This is the witness that separates the response-theoretic definition of EIT from the dark-state diagnosis. |

The 2g2e package is deliberately kept intact — code, data, figures and
checksums together — rather than split across `calculations/` and `results/`.
A witness that cannot be handed to someone as one directory is not much of a
witness.
