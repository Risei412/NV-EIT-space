## Problem

EIT in solid-state defect centers is conventionally diagnosed by the presence of
a dark state. This repository's work replaces that diagnosis with a
response-theoretic one — transparency as coherent cancellation in the observed
response generated by the control field, formalized through the sector cut
`χ − χ^(S)_cut` — and classifies systems by a suppression index
`ν ∈ {∞} ∪ (0,∞) ∪ {0}` (exact structural no-go / asymptotic no-go /
protected go).

What is unsettled is whether that classification is an *experimentally*
meaningful one or only a structural one. Gate E found that the platform the
theory was built for, optical NV, cannot have its exponent read at all.

## Current claim / hypothesis

Three claims, at different strengths:

1. **Structural (believed established).** For finite-dimensional Markovian
   weak-probe systems, the suppression index `ν` obeys an exclusive trichotomy,
   and the correct object for a no-go statement is the sector cut, not the full
   response — `χ_full = 0` is not a no-go (Theorem 2B note).
2. **Mechanism (believed established, newer).** Matched-readout transparency has
   a floor that is an *identity*, not a search result:
   `Re χ_matched = x†Γx ≥ 0` for `A(δ) = Γ + i(H − δI)`, `Γ = Γ† ≥ 0`. An exact
   zero requires `x ∈ ker Γ`, which matched readout cannot reach; the only
   escape is unmatched (source ≠ readout).
3. **Experimental (currently rejected in general form).** The claim of a
   "measurable design rule" across engineered-dissipation platforms does not
   survive Gate E. What survives is narrower: a finite window exists for a
   3-mode engineered-loss chain.

## Assumptions

- Finite-dimensional, Markovian, weak-probe. The trichotomy is stated in this
  regime and nowhere else.
- Passive coherence block `A(δ) = Γ + i(H − δI)` with `Γ = Γ† ≥ 0` for the
  matched-floor identity. `Γ > 0` gives strict positivity.
- **Load-bearing and known to be schematic:** the group-IV (SiV/SnV) phonon
  normalization and dipole geometry are schematic — single rate scale, orthogonal
  orbital basis legs, `|M0|` normalization. Constants are recorded in
  `GateC_material_independence/SiV_SnV_phonon_AIC_parameters.md` but were not
  refit. Integer exponents are unaffected; group-IV curves must not be read as
  quantitative predictions of absolute response.
- Gate E's 3-mode PASS carries design assumptions that are *not* demonstrated:
  15–45 MHz tunable engineered loss, coupling-preserving independent loss sweep,
  −190 dBm-class amplification and calibration, sign-switching transients, and a
  measured feedthrough drift spectrum.

## Open questions

- **Is there any platform where `ν` is both large enough to matter and small
  enough to measure?** `ν = 4` costs four decades of contrast per decade of `Γ`,
  which is what closes the NV window. The two requirements pull opposite ways;
  whether the tension is fundamental or an artifact of these platforms is open.
- **Does the sector-cut classification have an observable signature that is not
  the exponent?** If NV can only ever serve as a witness certifying an exact
  class, what certifies it?
- **What is the right genericity statement for T1 Theorem 2.3(ii)?** It is argued
  for a single Lorentzian pole but asserted for "generic `K(z)`" with no
  definition of the dense set. Either the claim weakens to "any passive response
  with a single Lorentzian pole" or genericity gets defined.
- **Can the unmatched escape route be characterized?** The identity says exact
  zeros need `x ∈ ker Γ`, reachable only when source ≠ readout, and F5B did find
  exact zeros in Raman-basis coherence readout. What is the general condition?

## Failure cases / counterexamples

- **Optical NV cannot resolve its own exponent.** The single most important
  finding of the 2026-08-02 audit, produced by the newly added G-D7 slope budget.
  Usable window 1.02 decade (single), 0.84 (post-selected shimmed), 0.51
  (high density) against 1 decade required.
- **Gate B's experimental window is unresolved.** At realistic κ the exponent is
  ≈ 0 for both generic and protected; integer asymptotics are readable only 8+
  orders above physical bath damping. Gate B's claim is structural universality,
  not a measurement procedure for that device.
- **`gate_F5B_full_gksl.json` is stale and self-contradictory.** The committed
  result predates a bug fix; on rerun the gate verdict `any_gain_in_matched`
  flips `true → false`, contradicting the `F5B_findings.md` shipped beside it.
- **Theorem 1B's proof has an unwritten step.** "A short rearrangement shows
  this equals …" hides a real subtlety: `L_μ|D⟩ = λ_μ|D⟩` gives
  `ρ_D L_μ† = λ_μ* ρ_D` but *not* `ρ_D L_μ = λ_μ* ρ_D`. A route via
  `H_eff := H − (i/2)Σ_μ L_μ†L_μ` avoids it.
- **T1 Theorem 2.1 is not proved in this repository** — applied verbatim from a
  package that is not present here. Theorem 2.2's counterexample numbers are
  generated by no code in the repository.
- **Manuscript Fig. 4 is a `quick=True` figure**, while the README instructs
  `quick=False`, which prints different values in the figure.
